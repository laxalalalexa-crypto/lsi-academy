To join the Luna Sharpshooter Infantry (LSI), recruits must first complete the academy's enrollment process. This begins with the formal Enlistment Form, followed by the Selection phase, where recruits are evaluated based on their discipline, communication, conduct, and overall capabilities. The process concludes with Final Verification, where recruits must complete the required verification procedures before officially entering the academy.

Once admitted, recruits will begin the Luna Sharpshooter Infantry Academy Curriculum, which is designed to develop disciplined and capable infantry personnel. The curriculum introduces recruits to the fundamental knowledge, responsibilities, procedures, and standards expected of an LSI personnel.

The academy curriculum consists of several progressive Phases, beginning with basic infantry fundamentals and continuing toward more advanced training. Recruits will be evaluated through written, practical, and oral assessments throughout their academy journey.

The curriculum will conclude with a Final Evaluation, where recruits must demonstrate the required knowledge, discipline, teamwork, communication, and overall performance expected of an LSI personnel.

Upon successful completion of the academy, qualified recruits may officially graduate and proceed to their assigned responsibilities within the Luna Sharpshooter Infantry. Further specialization, assignments, or advancement may be determined by academy and organizational leadership.

PROCEDURES FOR CERTIFICATE RENEWAL
FOR LSI PERSONNEL WITH UP-TO-DATE CERTIFICATES:
  • The certificate must correspond with the current Main Curriculum Version of the Luna Sharpshooter Infantry Academy.
  • Personnel must remain in good standing within LSI.
  • Personnel must not have received a Dishonorable Discharge from LSI.
  • Personnel must continue to comply with current academy and TFA regulations.
FOR LSI PERSONNEL WITH OUTDATED CERTIFICATES:
  • Personnel must complete the newly introduced requirements necessary for certificate renewal.
  • Requirements already completed under the previous curriculum do not need to be repeated unless specifically required by academy administration.
  • Personnel must remain in good standing within LSI.
  • Personnel must not have received a Dishonorable Discharge from LSI.
  • Personnel must comply with the current Main Curriculum Version and all updated academy requirements.
ACADEMY OBJECTIVE

The Luna Sharpshooter Infantry Academy aims to produce disciplined, professional, and capable infantry personnel who are prepared to fulfill their responsibilities within LSI and uphold the standards of TFA.
